4. Purposes and Legal Bases of Processing

We process personal data only for legitimate, specific and transparent purposes.

4.1. Project Inquiries and Professional Services

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, assess project requirements, prepare commercial proposals, communicate with prospective clients and provide architectural, interior design, project management and related services.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where processing is necessary to take pre-contractual steps requested by an individual or to perform a contract with that individual.

Where the relationship concerns a corporate client, relevant professional contact information may be processed on the basis of legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, where applicable.

4.2. Client and Supplier Relationships

We process relevant information to administer contracts, coordinate projects, communicate with suppliers and consultants, manage procurement, maintain business records and fulfil contractual obligations.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b), Article 6(1)(c) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, depending on the purpose and circumstances.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where applicable to pre-contractual recruitment measures, or another appropriate legal basis depending on the circumstances.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(a) GDPR where consent is required. Strictly necessary website security and operational processing may rely on Article 6(1)(f), where appropriate.
